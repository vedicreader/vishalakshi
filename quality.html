quality

Find junk documents with a noise score, record which sections were useful, and optionally fit a ranker from that record

A vault fills up with things that match many searches and answer none: cookie banners, site footers, navigation pages, licence boilerplate. This page describes three tools for that problem. All three are off until you turn them on.

  1. A noise score ranks the documents that are most likely junk. It needs no labels. On the project’s test corpora it scores 0.988 AUC, and 0.996 after it is fitted to a person’s marks. AUC is the area under the ROC curve. A score of 1.0 is a perfect ranking and 0.5 is chance. The score only suggests. A person reviews suggest_noisy, then marks documents with mark_noisy or accept_noisy.
  2. A feedback log records which sections an answer cited and which sections a person rated. It records them after learn() is on.
  3. A ranker is fitted from that log and reorders retrieval. No learned ranker beat plain reciprocal rank fusion in a repeatable way (evals/RESULTS.md). Leave the ranker off unless it helps on your own collection.

What was good

rate records one judgement about one section for one question. SIGNALS maps each signal to a label (1 good, 0 bad) and a weight.

signal label weight
up, good 1 3
down, bad 0 3
read: the person read the section 1 1.5
cited: an answer cited the section 1 1
shown: the section was offered and not cited 0 0.35

ratings returns the log, newest first.

Citations are the label

ask returns the sections its answer cited. After learn(), every ask calls log_ask. log_ask writes the cited sections to the log as positives and the other sections as weak negatives. An answer that cites nothing writes nothing, unless the caller passes up or down. The ranker and the document prior learn from these rows.

The Beta prior

doc_prior turns the log into a multiplier on the retrieval score of each document. It models the usefulness of a document as a Beta distribution. The weighted wins and losses of the document update the distribution. A document with little evidence stays near neutral. A document cited once does not move far ahead.

gamma sets how strongly the prior changes a score. explore is the chance, from 0 to 1, that doc_prior samples from the distribution (Thompson sampling) and does not use its mean. Sampling keeps documents with little evidence in play. lo and hi clamp every multiplier, so no amount of evidence can bury a document.

Noise candidates

suggest_noisy ranks the documents that look most like junk. It leaves out each document that a person has already judged for noise. mark_noisy records the decision. search, sections, context and ask leave out a document marked as noise. accept_noisy marks the current top suggestions in one call. mark_noisy_many marks a list of documents.

The features and the Ranker come from litesearch, which computes them from the chunk store alone. The 0.988 AUC is measured there. The features include these:

feature what it measures
hub how often a chunk appears among the nearest neighbours of other chunks
spread_chunk how evenly a chunk sits between topics
off_centre how far a chunk is from the middle of the collection
dup_out how much of a chunk is duplicated in other documents

This page adds the vault’s side. It adds the documents that a person marked and the questions each document was retrieved for (promiscuity). It also adds the rule that the score only suggests.

Learning from marks

The fixed weights need no labels. After a person marks a few documents as noisy and a few as worth keeping, fit_noise fits the weights to those marks. On held-out documents the fitted weights scored 0.996 AUC. They were fitted from six noisy documents and eighteen confirmed keepers (evals/RESULTS.md). A fitted blend changes nothing until you call use_noise(True).

The ranker

The ranker is a pairwise linear model trained on the feedback log. For each question it learns which of two sections ranks higher. fit_ranker(save=True) fits the ranker and stores it. use_ranker(True) is a separate call. It makes context use the ranker, and ask uses context. The ranker is off by default. In the measurements no learned reranker beat plain reciprocal rank fusion in a repeatable way.

The features

PAIR_FEATURES are the signals that exist when a section is retrieved. They are the retrieval score and rank, the gap to the top score, the words the section shares with the question (raw and weighted by rarity), the cosine similarity to the question, the section length, the document prior, the kind and age of the document, and the noise features. The method is in evals/.

Fitting, storing, switching on

Logging, fitting and switching on are separate calls. Nothing changes retrieval until you call use_ranker(True). learn() starts the log. training_data turns the log into rows grouped by question. fit_ranker and fit_noise fit the models. use_ranker and use_noise switch the stored models on. use_noise affects only suggest_noisy. evals/ holds the measurements on whether a collection should use a ranker.
